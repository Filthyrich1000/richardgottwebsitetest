# Welcome to My New Blog

This is a sample blog post created with the new content management system. You can now easily create and edit blog posts through a user-friendly interface at `/admin`.

## Features

- Easy visual editor
- Image uploads
- Categories and tags
- Automatic formatting

## How to Use

1. Go to `yoursite.com/admin`
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Click "New Blog Post"
4. Write your content
5. Publish!

Your posts will automatically appear on your blog page with your existing beautiful design.
